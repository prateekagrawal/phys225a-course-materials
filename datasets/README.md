# Samples for your analysis

Event files are stored separately from this repository. Find available samples
in FindingZ or in your notebook's sample table. Use the sample IDs and collider
settings specified in your assignment.

For each comparison, check the process, beam energy, simulation stage, generator
cuts, and detector settings. Signal and background samples used for counting must
have compatible configurations and cross-section normalization.

Keep the runs used in your analysis: a saved notebook does not embed their events.
If a required sample is missing, ask for its location before continuing.
