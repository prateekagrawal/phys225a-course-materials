# Optional capstone: what else could explain the data?

Not ready for release: the blackbox_1 catalog item is only a disabled placeholder.

Give students a fixed collider, known-background predictions and an unlabeled
pseudo-dataset. Require exploration, a frozen selection or fitting strategy,
comparison of at least two hypotheses, and confirmation using an independent
release. Supply a trials/look-elsewhere discussion if they scan many mass windows.

Before assignment: build and validate the hidden model, check consistency of its
allowed parameters and widths, generate and validate all relevant backgrounds,
choose a feasible luminosity and effect size, and test the whole analysis blind.
Keep the true model and answer key in instructor-only storage. Do not distribute a
readable UFO and describe its parameters as hidden.

Deliverable: saved analysis notebook, generation provenance for hypothesis samples,
a quantitative comparison and an account of what remains undetermined.
Collider optimization and jet reclustering are deferred to 225B.
